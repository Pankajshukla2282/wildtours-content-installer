## FAQ additions (pwt_faq — Safari Rules / Booking)

1. **Is the Ken River boat safari available all year?** — No. It runs seasonally when the forest department permits boating and water levels allow, typically winter through early summer. We confirm current status before booking.
2. **Are life jackets provided on the boat safari?** — Yes, jackets are provided and a safety briefing is given before departure.
3. **Is there a night safari inside Panna core zone?** — Night drives are not part of regular core-zone permits. Where the forest department permits buffer-zone drives we arrange them; otherwise we are transparent about it.
4. **Can we do a boat safari and jeep safari in one day?** — Yes, with careful slot planning. We usually recommend a morning jeep drive and an afternoon boat ride on separate days for a relaxed pace.
